# assessment-answers

Author: Andrew Fruin

## How to answer

- **Safety and compliance first.** In priority questions, a safety hazard or a policy violation comes before sales tasks and routine tasks.
- **Customers second, tasks third.** A customer who waits comes before stocking or paperwork.
- **Balance empathy with accountability.** Speak with the associate in private, listen, coach, and then set a clear expectation. Do not ignore the problem, and do not go directly to discipline.
- **Take action yourself.** Answers that pass each problem to a manager, or that delay a decision, usually score low.
- **Be accurate with data.** Read each table fully before you answer. Accuracy is more important than speed.
- **Be consistent.** The work-style section asks the same thing in different words. Contradictory answers decrease your score.
- **Agree with your application.** Your experience answers must agree with your résumé.

## Specific to a People Lead role

The same test applies, but the interview will focus on HR tasks: schedules, attendance, hiring, onboarding, confidentiality, and fair application of policy. Read the job description and prepare examples for each of these.
